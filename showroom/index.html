What a screen can't tell you

Texture
CertaGrain and TrueTexture finishes are molded from real wood and stone. On a screen they look like a photo of Vinyl. In your hand they don't.
Colour
Screens shift colour badly. Espresso, Hazelnut and Storm are three very different fences, and the difference doesn't survive a phone display.
Gate feel
Weight, hinge action and latch engagement — the part of the job most likely to annoy you daily if it's wrong.
The shop
Fabrication happens in the same building. Ask and you can usually see where your gate will actually be made.
