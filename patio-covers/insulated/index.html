An insulated panel is a foam core bonded between aluminum skins, and the difference in July is not subtle. If the patio is used as a room rather than a walkway, this is the upgrade that earns its cost.

Insulated panels also span further with fewer posts, which usually means a cleaner view out from the house.

Demolition and haul-away of the existing structure are included, and you get a written itemised price you can compare line by line against other bids.
