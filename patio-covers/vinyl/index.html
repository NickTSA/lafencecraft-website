Vinyl patio covers suit properties where the cover should read as part of the house rather than as a structure bolted onto it, and they never need repainting.

They pair naturally with vinyl fencing and railing, which is worth considering if you are doing more than one project.

Demolition and haul-away of the existing structure are included, and you get a written itemised price you can compare line by line against other bids.
