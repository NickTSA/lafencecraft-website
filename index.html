Common questions

Before you call anyone

The questions we're asked most, answered straight. If yours isn't here, ask us at the walkthrough.

See all questions
How much does a new fence cost in the Los Angeles area?

Price is driven by three things: linear footage, material, and how much demolition and haul-away the old fence needs. Wood is the least expensive per foot and Vinyl and Aluminum sit above it, with Composite typically highest. Slope, hard digging in decomposed granite, and gates all add cost. We give a written, itemised price after measuring on site — not a per-foot guess over the phone.

Do I need a permit for a fence?

It depends on height and location on the lot, and the rules vary between cities and even between parcels. Rear and side yard fencing at standard residential height is often exempt, while taller fencing, street-facing fencing, and anything structural like a patio cover more often is not. Los Angeles also has designated Fence Height Districts that change what's allowed. We check your parcel before you sign.

How long does installation take?

A typical residential fence replacement is one to two days on site once materials are in. Custom gates, automation, patio covers and decks run longer. Lead time on materials is usually the bigger variable, and we give you a date range in writing with the estimate.

Do you handle the demolition of my old fence?

Yes. Removal, haul-away and disposal of the existing fence are included in the quote unless you tell us you want to keep it. There's no separate disposal charge added at the end.

What warranty do you provide?

Materials carry the manufacturer warranty, which on most of our Vinyl and Aluminum lines is a limited lifetime warranty. Our workmanship is warrantied directly by us — if something we installed fails, you call us and we handle it, with no third party in the middle.
