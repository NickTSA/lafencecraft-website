Burbank questions

Permits, timing and local rules

Do you need a permit for a fence in Burbank?

It depends on the height and where on the lot the fence sits, and Burbank sets its own rules. Rear and side yard fencing at standard residential height is frequently exempt while taller or street-facing fencing often is not. We check the requirements for your specific parcel before you sign anything, and we pull the permit where one is needed.

How quickly can you get to a job in Burbank?

Burbank is roughly 28 minutes from our Rosemead showroom, so we can usually get an estimator out within a few days. Install dates depend on material lead time, which we give you in writing with the estimate.

What kind of fencing do you install most in Burbank?

Flat, regular lots with alley access — straightforward runs where the value is in material choice and a clean gate rather than complex site work.

Do you handle removal of the old fence?

Yes — demolition, haul-away and disposal are included in the quoted price.
