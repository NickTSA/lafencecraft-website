About us

A fence company with a fabrication shop

Seven years, 5,600+ projects, and Aluminum, Vinyl and Composite systems built in our own facility rather than ordered in.

Home / About

Craft over catalogue

The name isn't decoration. All of our Aluminum, Vinyl and Composite systems are manufactured entirely in-house, which gives us control over precision, finish and customisation that a company reselling someone else's panels simply doesn't have.

It also means we can build to an opening rather than around one. Odd widths, sloped runs, a gate that has to clear a driveway apron — these are fabrication problems, and we have a shop.

Shop, showroom and office are all at the Rosemead address. The samples you handle on the showroom floor came off our own line, and the crew that builds your gate is in the same building.

Every job gets a written, itemised estimate. Demolition and haul-away are included rather than added at the end. And the workmanship warranty is ours directly, not routed through a third party, so a warranty call goes to the same number you called the first time.

We moved our showroom to Rosemead in 2026 after years operating out of Duarte. The team, the crews and the license are the same — the front door is just closer to the middle of our service area now.
